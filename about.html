Who We Are

About Shelfie

Welcome to Shelfie, an online bookstore created for people who love reading and learning. Our aim is to make it easier for customers to discover and purchase books from the comfort of their own homes.

Our Story

In 2020, Shelfie was created with the aim of making books more accessible and easier to purchase online. Shelfie is an online bookstore that offers a variety of books for different readers and interests.

What We Offer

Shelfie offers different types of books, including Educational, Story, Fiction, Self-Development, Historical, Romance and Decorational books. This gives our customers different options to choose from.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make finding and buying books simple and convenient. We want to encourage people to read, learn and discover new ideas through books.

Why Choose Shelfie?

Shelfie provides customers with a convenient way to browse different types of books in one place. Whether you are looking for education, entertainment or personal development, Shelfie gives you the opportunity to explore books that match your interests.

Our Goal

Our goal is to grow Shelfie into a trusted online bookstore where readers can easily find books that interest them. We want to create a positive reading experience and encourage everyone to Read. Learn. Grow. Succeed.
